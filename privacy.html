Oddly True Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-04

Oddly True Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Oddly True" to upload the channel's own original animated videos and to read the channel's own analytics.

Data accessed

The OAuth token for the Oddly True channel, the channel's video metadata, and its analytics reports.

How it is used

Uploading videos; setting titles, descriptions and thumbnails; measuring the performance of our own videos.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and analytics are stored only on the owner's private computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored analytics are deleted on request or when the tool is retired.
